\textbf{Software.} Grid \texttt{e2d607f}, built with grid-benchmark \texttt{c7457a8} (the UK NNSS
benchmark harness); GCC 14.1.0 (\texttt{gnu\_comp/14.1.0}), Open\,MPI 5.0.3 (one rank,
\texttt{--mpi 1.1.1.1}), libraries (GMP, MPFR, HDF5, FFTW, LIME) from the harness's pixi env;
LIKWID 5.5.1. Grid flags: \texttt{-O3 -fopenmp -march=znver2} / \texttt{-march=sapphirerapids},
\texttt{--enable-simd=AVX2}/\texttt{AVX512}, \texttt{--enable-comms=mpi-auto}; full flags in each run's
\texttt{build-flags.txt}. % TODO(A): clang build row if tune-clang ran
Benchmark\_Grid runs with the brief's flags (FP64, sp4-2as, sp4-f and su4 benchmarks off), so its
GFLOP/s are single precision.
\textbf{Pinning.} likwid-bench: \texttt{-W N:...} on physical cores (compact: socket\,0 first;
scatter: equal per NUMA domain). Grid: \texttt{OMP\_NUM\_THREADS}=$T$, \texttt{OMP\_PROC\_BIND=close},
\texttt{OMP\_PLACES=cores}; under likwid-perfctr, \texttt{-C N:0-}$(T{-}1)$. On DINE2 the OS numbers
socket\,0 cores even and socket\,1 odd, so \texttt{close} with $T\le32$ still spans both sockets.

\begin{center}\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3pt}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrlrrr@{}}\toprule
Node & GFLOP/s & Build & $L$ & $T$ & \% SP peak\\\midrule
DINE  & 361 & gcc, close/cores & 48 & 32 & 11.8\\
DINE2 & 740 & clang, close/cores & 24 & 64 & 8.6\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
Both use first-touch placement; interleaving pages ($-$14 to $-$23\,\%), SMT (DINE $-$30\,\%) and
spreading a half node onto one socket all lose, as expected for a bandwidth-bound kernel. Clang beats gcc
by 7--47\,\% on DINE2 only. % TODO(A): gc007 best-node result; cache-resident L<=16 maxima (848 / 2677).
